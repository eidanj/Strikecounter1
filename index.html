Play

Team Deathmatch

Tactical rounds. Tortellini vs Counter-Tortellini. Teammate bots don't respawn mid-round — last team standing wins the round.

Free-For-All

Everyone for themselves. First to 20 eliminations wins. Bots respawn quickly; you respawn after 20s.

Settings

Sprint is triggered by double-tapping W. In Hold mode you sprint while W stays held. In Toggle mode sprint stays on until you double-tap again, stop, crouch, aim or run out of energy.

Energy: sprinting drains the energy bar. Below 6 you're exhausted and can't sprint until it recovers. It refills slowly while walking and fastest standing still.

Stealth: enemies need line of sight. Crouch behind a wall, car, crate or low stone wall and they lose track of you. Bushes and vines hide you too (but bullets go straight through leaves).

Focus: bots already in a firefight stay locked on their current target — a great moment to flank.

Accuracy: stand still or crouch for tight shots. Moving and jumping widen your crosshair. Control recoil by pulling down while spraying.

Friendly fire is off — your rounds pass through teammates. Headshots deal 4x damage. Backstabs with the Stiletto hit extra hard.

About

Strike Counter 1 is an affectionate parody of tactical shooters, set in the sun-baked Italian town of Bellavista and the vineyards beyond its walls.

Everything you see and hear — buildings, trees, textures, guns, sounds — is generated procedurally in your browser with three.js and the Web Audio API.

No real-world brands were harmed. No gore: eliminated players turn red, tip over, and vanish in a puff of smoke.
